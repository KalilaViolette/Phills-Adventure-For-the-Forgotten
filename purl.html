Purl, a test run. Move: arrows / left stick (up aims the hook higher, down drops through a twig). Jump: Z / Space / Cross. Attack: X / R1 (again to swing back the other way; hold it for the slam; up for the uppercut, which leaves her spinning; down for the low sweep, or in the air the down swing that bounces off what it hits). Dodge: Shift / Circle (in the air it turns her the way you push, at full speed; attack out of it for a slash, or push back the way you came for the slam; dodge a lunge at the last moment and the next attack is the counter; swing as something lunges to clash). Hook: C / R2 (tap to yank, hold to swing). Sew: hold V / Square standing still. Right stick (Q / E) turns her to swing one way while backing off the other. R / Select: back to the start.
