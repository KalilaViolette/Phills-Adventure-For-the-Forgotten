# Phill's Adventure: For the Forgotten

My Phill Metroidvania.

Phill wakes up on a sunny day, finds the door open and waddles out into the backyard. A crow snatches him up and he ends up lost in the park, which feels like a huge dark forest when you're a little plush. Something's hurting everyone who lives there, so he stays to help before finding his way home.

**Play it here:** https://kalilaviolette.github.io/Phills-Adventure-For-the-Forgotten/

It's still being built, so expect rough spots. It's best with a controller (PlayStation layout), but it works on keyboard too. If your controller doesn't show up, press a button on it once the game is open.

## Controls

| | Controller | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left stick or D-pad | Arrow keys or WASD |
| Jump | X | Z or Space |
| Swing (once he has the sword) | R1 | X |
| Shield (once he has it) | L1, or point the right stick | V or Shift |
| Roll (once he figures it out) | Circle | C |
| Squeak (once he learns it) | Triangle | F |
| Spin (once he learns it) | Square | G |
| Map | Share | M |
| Look up or down | Hold up or down | Hold up or down |

Walk into a low gap to crawl under it. Fall past a ledge and hold toward it to grab on, then press up to climb. Down and jump drops through shelves.

There's also a tuning menu (Tab or Options) and a hitbox view (H), left in from testing.

## Running it yourself

There's no build step. Download the repo and open `index.html` in Chrome. The `art`, `js` and `rooms` folders need to stay next to it.
