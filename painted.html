The painted rooms, a test

The whole game, with Rootwood's six painted rooms in it: under the oak, the squirrel's hollow, the bear's home, the litter path, the mushroom well and the bramble thicket. Under the oak is newly painted, lit by the game. Pick one under Go to. The hollow is lit by what is in it now: the mushroom, small fungi and the daylight down the knothole. This page keeps its own saves, the main game's aren't touched. Add ?room=hollow (or roots, bearhome, litterpath, well, bramble) to the address to start in that room.
